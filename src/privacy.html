Privacy

Updated 26 November 2026

MathTime is a math game at math.nshanklin.com. You can play without an account. This page says what MathTime keeps when you make one, and why.

What an account keeps

  • Your name, the one you choose. It shows on the boards.
  • Your password, stored only as a one-way hash, never as typed.
  • Your games: each finished level and daily challenge, with its time and misses.
  • A log-in cookie, so you stay signed in. The server keeps only a hash of it.

Signing in with Google

MathTime asks Google for the openid scope only. From Google it keeps one thing: the account id Google gives you, so it knows it's you next time. It does not get or keep your email address, your real name or your photo.

On this device

Your settings and progress are also saved in your browser's local storage, so the game remembers them between visits. Clearing your browser's data for the site removes them.

What it doesn't do

  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone. Your data stays on MathTime's own server.

Logs and fonts

The web server keeps a standard access log (IP address, time, page asked for), used only to keep the site running and to stop abuse. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address, as for any website.

Deleting your account

Open a GitHub issue with your MathTime name, and your account, games and Google link will be deleted.

Questions

Ask in a GitHub issue. If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
