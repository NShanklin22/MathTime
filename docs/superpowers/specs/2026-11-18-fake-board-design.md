# Made-up players on the daily board

Decided 2026-11-18, in conversation. The owner asked for made-up names and times on the leaderboard, enough
that the board never looks empty, most of them easy to beat, and pushed off as real players come. Their
choice: the made-up players look real, with no mark. The rest are the recommended defaults.

## 1. Who they are
- Ten a day, made by the server from the date alone, so every caller sees the same ten and nothing is
  stored. Each date has its own names and times.
- Names come from a list of plain handles (letters, digits, `_` and `-`, 3 to 20 long, as sign-up allows).
  A name a real account holds, in any case, is never used: a spare handle takes its place, with the same
  score, and the other rows stay as they were.
- None on a date past tomorrow, which no one can have played.

## 2. Their scores
- Most are easy to beat. Of the ten: one fast (0:58 to 1:10), two middling (1:15 to 1:50), five slow
  (1:50 to 4:00), and two out (one between questions 9 and 17, one between 4 and 12).
- They rank with real scores by the board's usual rules: finishers by time, then outs by how far they got.
  On a tie, the real player goes above.

## 3. Pushed off
- Each real score on the date takes one made-up player off: the worst-placed one left. With ten real
  scores, none are left.
- "N played" counts the made-up players still shown, and so does every place ("5th of 9").

## 4. Not in this piece
Made-up players on the level boards (there are none yet); changing the board's look.
